# Dad Joke Showdown — 要求与候选

## 官方要求（核对自 make.fun 公开接口，2026-11-25）
- slug: u-mfnakmal-dad-joke-showdown-1d2ce3e5
- 形式：单个 dad joke（爸爸笑话），最多 200 字符，笑话本身就是全部参赛内容。
- 评判：评委模型两两对比，看哪句更像一个成功的 dad joke——双关（pun）、让人扶额的 groan、以及纸面上的一本正经（straight-faced delivery）。私下轮次评判，决赛胜出者拿头奖。
- 截止：2026-11-25T20:25:00.000Z。

## 候选
1. Why did the scarecrow get promoted? He was outstanding in his field — unlike me, who is outstanding in everyone else's meetings.
2. I'm reading a book about anti-gravity. It's impossible to put down, which is also what my kids say about my jokes at dinner.
3. I used to hate facial hair, but then it grew on me. Same with these jokes, according to nobody in my family, ever.
4. Why don't scientists trust atoms? Because they make up everything — including, my children insist, most of my stories.
5. What do you call a fish without eyes? A fsh. My kids have requested I stop calling them anything at all.
6. I told my suitcase there would be no vacation this year. Now I'm dealing with emotional baggage.

## 选定
第 3 句。"grew on me" 是 dad joke 的经典双关，后半句把家人的 groan 直接写进笑话里，一本正经、扶额感拉满，且不是烂大街的现成段子原文。

## 修订（2026-11-25，审核后）
修订原因：审核指出原选定句的前半句 "I used to hate facial hair, but then it grew on me" 是流传极广的现成 dad joke 原句，原创性不足（上文"不是烂大街现成段子"的说法对前半句不成立）。按要求保留后半句"家人 groan"的尾巴（"Same with these jokes, according to nobody in my family, ever."），只换前半句，双关全部现造、不用流传现成的。

新前半句候选：
1. I used to hate doing laundry, but I've finally come clean about it.（come clean：一语双关——坦白认错 + 把衣服洗干净）
2. I used to think my garden hose was out to get me, but we've worked out the kinks.（kinks：水管的扭结 + 彼此的隔阂）
3. I used to think my treadmill was a waste of money, but now I'm really running with it.（running with it：在跑步机上跑 + 顺势接受）
4. I used to hate building flat-pack furniture, but it's finally coming together.（coming together：家具拼起来了 + 事情有起色）
5. I used to think my robot vacuum was lazy, but it really cleaned up its act.（cleaned up its act：改过自新 + 本职就是打扫）
6. I used to think my sourdough starter was a flop, but it rose to the occasion.（rose：面团发酵升起 + 不负众望）

新选定：第 1 句。come clean 的双关最扶额、语气最一本正经，"从讨厌到坦然接受"的弧线与尾巴的 "Same with these jokes" 衔接最顺。
新完整句：I used to hate doing laundry, but I've finally come clean about it. Same with these jokes, according to nobody in my family, ever.（130 字符）
